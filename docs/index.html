What this app does

Portfolio Calendar Pull is a personal tool that connects to your Google Calendar, pulls your scheduled events, and organises them into a consolidated portfolio view so you can see how your time is allocated across projects and commitments.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account and grant access to your calendar.
  • The app reads your calendar events through the Google Calendar API.
  • Events are grouped and summarised into a portfolio view.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Google account access

Portfolio Calendar Pull requests a single Google OAuth scope, and uses it only for the purpose described below.

ScopeWhy it is requested
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly Read your calendars and their events, so the app can build the portfolio view. This scope is read-only: it gives the app no ability to create, change or delete anything in your calendar.

Portfolio Calendar Pull's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
