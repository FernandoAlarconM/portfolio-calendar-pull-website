This Privacy Policy explains how Portfolio Calendar Pull ("the app", "we") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when you connect your Google account. The app is operated by Fernando Alarcón. If you do not agree with this policy, do not authorise the app.

1. Who we are

  • Application name: Portfolio Calendar Pull
  • Operator: Fernando Alarcón (individual developer)
  • Contact: fernando.alarcon.agent@gmail.com

2. Google user data we access

The app accesses Google user data only through the Google Calendar API, and only after you grant consent on the Google OAuth consent screen. We request a single scope:

ScopeData accessedPurpose
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly The list of calendars in your account (names, IDs, time zones), and the events in them: title, description, location, start and end times, attendees, recurrence and event IDs. To read your events and build the consolidated portfolio view, attributing each event to the correct calendar and respecting your calendar time zones.

3. How we use your data

Google user data is used exclusively to provide the features you see in the app:

  • Retrieving your calendar events and displaying them in a consolidated portfolio view.
  • Grouping and summarising events so you can see how your time is allocated.

The scope requested is read-only. The app cannot and does not create, modify or delete events, calendars or any other data in your Google account.

We do not use your Google user data for any other purpose.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We do not transfer your data to third parties, except as required by law or as strictly necessary to provide the app (see section 6).
  • We do not use your data for advertising, ad targeting, remarketing or personalisation.
  • We do not use your data to train, fine-tune or improve generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • We do not allow humans to read your data, except with your explicit prior consent for a specific support request, when required by law, or where the data is aggregated and anonymised for security or abuse prevention.

5. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the machine running the app, in an access-restricted location. They are never transmitted to any third party.
  • Calendar data is held only for as long as needed to produce the portfolio view. Any cached copy is stored locally and is not published or shared.
  • All communication with Google APIs uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access is limited to the account owner who authorised the app.

6. Sharing and sub-processors

The app does not share your Google user data with third parties. The only external service involved is Google itself, which provides the Calendar API and the authentication service. We may disclose data if legally compelled to do so by a valid legal process.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Retention: calendar data is retained only while your authorisation is active and only for the purpose of producing the portfolio view.
  • Revoking access: you may revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately stops all further access to your calendar.
  • Deletion: when you revoke access or request deletion, stored tokens and any cached calendar data are deleted within 30 days.
  • Deletion requests: email fernando.alarcon.agent@gmail.com and we will confirm deletion within 30 days.

8. Limited Use disclosure

Portfolio Calendar Pull's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact fernando.alarcon.agent@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent is also possible directly through your Google Account permissions page.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes affecting how Google user data is handled will be communicated before they take effect.
