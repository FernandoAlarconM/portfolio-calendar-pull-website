Portfolio Calendar Pull

Reads events from your Google Calendar and consolidates them into a single portfolio view.

What this app does

Portfolio Calendar Pull is a personal tool that connects to your Google Calendar, pulls your scheduled events, and organises them into a consolidated portfolio view so you can see how your time is allocated across projects and commitments.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account and grant access to your calendar.
  • The app reads your calendar events through the Google Calendar API.
  • Events are grouped and summarised into a portfolio view.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Google account access

Portfolio Calendar Pull requests the following Google OAuth scopes. Each one is used only for the purpose described below.

ScopeWhy it is requested
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly Read your calendar events to build the portfolio view.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly Read the list of your calendars so events can be attributed to the correct calendar.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar Create or update events when you explicitly ask the app to write back to your calendar.
openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Identify your account so the app can sign you in and keep your data separate.

Portfolio Calendar Pull's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
